# MASE Discovery & Growth Plan

**Property:** MASE at `virtualmase.vercel.app/mase.html`  
**Owner:** Mason Nguyen  
**Initial audience:** Boutique advisors, independent sponsors, small private-equity operating teams, and corporate-development leaders who have AI interest but no clear operating model.  
**Purpose:** Create qualified conversations about governed M&A AI workflows—not vanity traffic, generic AI consulting leads, or automated outreach volume.

## Positioning to test

> **MASE helps M&A teams turn AI curiosity into governed, repeatable workflows—with approved data boundaries, human review, and an intelligible record.**

The message should lead with the buyer’s operating concern: a team is already experimenting with AI but does not know which work is safe, useful, or worth formalizing. It should not lead with models, protocols, autonomous agents, “transformation,” or a request for confidential deal material.

## Six-week discovery sequence

| Week | Published asset or action | Reader value | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish the MASE prototype and index it through the site sitemap. | A concise explanation of controlled M&A AI adoption. | Page is reachable, consistent, and understood by a small set of trusted reviewers. |
| 2 | Publish a short LinkedIn field note: **“AI for Deal Teams: Start With the Workflow, Not the Tool.”** | Gives operators a concrete starting frame without selling a platform. | Relevant practitioners reply with a named workflow or an adoption concern. |
| 3 | Publish a one-page *Controlled Deal-Team AI Starter Map*. | Provides a reusable checklist for scoping a first workflow. | Prospects request the checklist or ask to discuss its application. |
| 4 | Hold five to ten research conversations with the selected buyer profile. | Lets participants articulate their actual workflow, data, and governance constraints. | Repeated language and pain points emerge; at least one prospect asks about a working session. |
| 5 | Offer a paid MASE readiness session. | Gives a sponsor a fixed, low-risk entry point. | A qualified prospect commits budget to alignment and workflow selection. |
| 6 | Publish an anonymized learning note only if an observation can be safely stated. | Shows the operating method without revealing client or deal information. | A second buyer recognizes a similar problem and requests a conversation. |

## Initial outreach copy

### Personal introduction

> I am developing MASE, a practical AI operating model for M&A teams. The focus is not autonomous deal judgment—it is helping teams choose one useful workflow, set data and review boundaries, and build a pattern people can actually use. I am speaking with a small number of practitioners to understand where adoption is getting stuck. If this is relevant to your team, would you be open to a 25-minute research conversation? I am not asking for confidential deal information.

### Follow-up after a relevant conversation or post

> Your point about **[specific workflow or concern]** is exactly the adoption gap MASE is studying. I have published a short framework for starting with a bounded workflow and explicit review controls. If useful, I can send it over; I would also value a brief perspective on whether the framework misses a real deal-team constraint.

## Conversation guide

The first call is discovery, not a product demonstration. Ask what part of the deal lifecycle creates the most repeatable knowledge-work burden; what tools are already approved; what information cannot leave a designated environment; where senior reviewers lose confidence; and what evidence would justify continuing a pilot. Do not request proprietary files, names of live deals, or material nonpublic information.

## Measurement snapshot

| Measure | Definition | First six-week target |
|---|---|---:|
| Qualified conversations | A discussion with an M&A-adjacent decision-maker who can name a workflow or adoption constraint. | 5–10 |
| Workflow specificity | Conversations that produce a clearly described, bounded workflow. | At least 60% of qualified conversations |
| Paid-entry conversion | Readiness sessions or scoped diagnostic engagements sold. | 1–2 |
| Evidence quality | Usable, nonconfidential learning that can improve the offer or content. | One documented learning memo per week |
| Reputation risk | Misunderstandings, correction requests, or signals the offering overstates its scope. | Record and resolve every instance |

## Learning rules

A high volume of impressions without qualified conversations is not success. If readers describe MASE as a generic AI consultancy, revise the message toward a specific deal-team workflow. If they associate it with autonomous investment or legal decisions, make the human-control boundary more visible. If buyers ask repeatedly for the same template, review pattern, or knowledge workflow, that is evidence for the next productized component—not an instruction to build an expansive platform immediately.

## Sources informing the audience problem

Current M&A research supports the demand hypothesis but does not validate virtualmase-specific demand.
