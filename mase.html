MASE / M&A AI systems

Make AI useful before you make it autonomous.

MASE helps M&A teams move from AI curiosity to governed, repeatable workflows. We start with the real operating friction, define the data and review boundaries, and leave a system your team can understand and use.

01 / The method

From curiosity to a workflow your team can defend.

MASE is not a generic AI demo. It applies an operating-systems approach to one bounded work problem at a time, with a clear accountable owner at every stage.

01

Map the work

Identify the manual workflow, user roles, source materials, bottlenecks, and the specific decision that must remain human.

02

Set the boundary

Define approved data sources, tools, access conditions, output limits, human reviewers, and escalation path before use expands.

03

Build the pattern

Create the knowledge, prompts, templates, and review criteria that make the workflow repeatable rather than dependent on individual experimentation.

04

Observe and improve

Record adoption, reviewer findings, output limits, and user feedback. Expand only when the evidence supports it.

02 / Control layer

Capability needs a context, a control, and a record.

A useful workflow stays legible to the people responsible for the deal. MASE makes the safety and accountability conditions part of the operating design.

Data boundary

Approved context

Specify which materials and sources may inform the workflow. Do not treat a consumer AI workspace as a default destination for sensitive deal information.

Review boundary

Named humans

Identify who checks important outputs, what they check for, and when a draft must be escalated rather than relied upon.

Action boundary

Bounded execution

Begin with preparation and decision-support work. Keep consequential decisions, disclosures, and external actions under explicit human authority.

Evidence boundary

Visible limits

Keep a practical record of inputs, prompts or rules, output review, exceptions, and the conditions under which the workflow should stop.

03 / Start small, learn quickly

The first engagement should create an operating baseline.

Begin with a fixed scope. The right next investment depends on the workflow the team actually uses—not a generic platform roadmap.

Entry / alignment

MASE readiness session

A focused working session for senior sponsors and operators who need an intelligible place to begin.

  • Workflow selection
  • AI opportunity and risk map
  • Initial control model

Implementation / practice

Deal-team operating sprint

A fixed-scope engagement to configure one or two governed AI workflow patterns and enable the people who will use them.

  • Workflow and data boundary
  • Reusable operating kit
  • Review and measurement baseline

04 / Scope discipline

Clear about what MASE is—and is not.

Trust begins with a precise boundary. MASE supports controlled AI adoption; it does not transfer accountability from the professionals responsible for the work.

MASE is

A systems-led approach to workflow mapping, controlled AI enablement, team practice, and evidence-based iteration for M&A practitioners.

MASE is not

Investment advice, legal advice, a virtual data room replacement, a claim of guaranteed outcomes, or an autonomous decision-maker for a deal team.

05 / Start a conversation

What is the first workflow your team needs to trust?

Bring the process, not confidential deal materials. The first conversation is for deciding whether the problem is a fit and defining an appropriate boundary.
